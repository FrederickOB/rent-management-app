# Privacy notice for tenants: outline

> **This is an outline, not a privacy notice. Do not publish it or show it to tenants.**
>
> It lists the headings a tenant-facing notice needs, each with one line on what goes under it, taken from what the product actually does. The wording, the legal bases and every legal statement are for counsel (OQ-53, OQ-14). So is who the notice speaks for: the landlord as controller, the platform, or both (OQ-50).

- **Status:** M11 (decision 5). No privacy notice exists in the product yet. Nothing in the tenant app, the links pages or the lease templates points to one; adding the link once the notice exists is a gap for the build.
- **Covers:** individual tenants, their contacts (guarantors, next of kin, employers) and a company tenant's signatories, who use the tenant app, the links pages, WhatsApp, SMS and email.
- **Source of the facts:** `docs/data-protection.md` (records of processing).

## Headings

1. **Who is responsible for your information.** The landlord or agency named on your lease, which decides how your tenancy data is used. Also the platform that runs the app and links for them, and its role (OQ-50).
2. **What this notice covers.** The tenant app, the pay, signing, notice and receipt links, messages on WhatsApp, SMS, email and push, and your lease records.
3. **The information held about you.** Name, phone and WhatsApp number, email, occupation, your contacts' details, lease terms, invoices, payments and receipts, messages, notices, signatures, photos and voice notes you send, and an identity check's result. A company tenant also has its registration details and TIN.
4. **Where it comes from.** You; the landlord's staff; your lease; the people you name as contacts; the payment provider; the identity provider; the messages you send.
5. **Why it is used, and on what basis.** One line per purpose: your tenancy, billing and receipts, reminders, legal notices, repairs and inspections, deposits, tax records the landlord must keep. The legal basis for each is for counsel.
6. **Messages and your choices.**
   - Business messages go only on a channel you agreed to.
   - Reply STOP, use the unsubscribe link in an email, or turn off notifications in the app.
   - Codes you ask for are always sent.
   - Legal notices follow your lease's electronic-service clause and are otherwise served in person (OQ-10, OQ-57).
7. **Identity checks.** Done by a live check against NIA's records through the identity provider. A photo or copy of your Ghana Card is never asked for or kept. The number, if recorded, is stored encrypted and shown masked (OQ-39).
8. **Payments.** Made through Paystack, which receives your payment details. Rent goes straight to the landlord's or agency's account. The platform never holds it.
9. **Signing and evidence.** What is kept when you sign: your typed or drawn signature, the time, the phone or email your code went to, your device and internet address, and a fingerprint of the document. For a notice, signing confirms you received it, not that you agree.
10. **Photos, videos and voice notes.** What you send with a repair request or in a message is copied to the landlord's records and used for that repair or inspection.
11. **Who sees it, and who it is shared with.**
    - The landlord's staff, each by their role; a caretaker sees only your name, phone and unit.
    - The service providers that carry messages and payments: Meta (WhatsApp), Hubtel (SMS), Postmark (email), Expo (app notifications), Paystack, the identity provider, and the hosting provider.
    - A landlord never sees what you hold with another landlord.
    - Nothing is sold or used for marketing.
12. **Where it is stored, and transfers abroad.** The country of hosting is decided at the first deployment (ADR-009). What must be declared, and what must stay in Ghana, is for counsel (OQ-14, OQ-49).
13. **How long it is kept.** Financial and notice records for a period the law sets (6 years is unverified), and everything else for periods counsel has not yet supplied. Leave the figures out until they are verified (OQ-14).
14. **How it is protected.** In plain words: encryption of ID and account numbers, access by role, a record of who saw or changed what, and secure links that expire.
15. **Your rights, and how to use them.**
    - Ask the landlord to see a copy, correct it, erase it or object to its use.
    - What happens: the request is recorded, and messages stop at once on an erasure request.
    - The limits: records the law requires to be kept are kept.
    - The response time is for counsel (OQ-65).
16. **Complaints.** How to complain to the landlord, and to the Data Protection Commission. The Commission's contact details and the wording are for counsel.
17. **Changes to this notice.** Its version and date, and how you will be told of a new one.
18. **Contact.** The landlord's or agency's contact for privacy questions, and the platform's.

## Where the notice must appear (for the build, once it exists)

- **The tenant app:** sign-in and profile.
- **Every links page:** a footer link, kept within the 1 MB budget.
- **The lease templates:** beside the electronic-service clause.
- **The first message that asks for consent,** on each channel.
