## 4. Design

### 4.1 Modules

- **`reporting`** (layer 8, new), lint map updated. It covers:
    - fee posting hooks (through billing's `afterReceipt`, beside rent tax);
    - statements;
    - remittances;
    - dashboards and the rollup;
    - Gate 1 metrics;
    - the compliance list.
- **The posting functions** for P14, P16 and P30 live in `packages/domain/src/ledger` and go through `PostingService`.

### 4.2 Fees (P14)

- **`management_agreement` gains** a fee kind (none, percentage or fixed per unit), the rate in basis points or the fixed amount in pesewas, and an effective date. A change is a new version, through the existing approval.
- **Per receipt:** the fee is the rate × the receipt's fee base (decision 2). It is rounded half up once, in `packages/domain/money`. The idempotency key is `P14:<payment>`.
- **When it is reversed:** a reversal or a refunded advance reverses it in proportion (decisions 2 and 3).
- **Monthly fixed fees:** `reporting.fixed-fees` runs on the last day of the month, per owner and unit, keyed `P14:<owner>:<unit>:<month>`.

### 4.3 Statements

- **Data:**
    - `owner_statement` records the owner, the month, its status (draft or issued), the template version, the document and its hash, and who issued it;
    - `owner_statement_figure` holds the figures as issued, append-only. Regeneration compares against them.
- **The query layer:**
    - `sumLines` gains an accounting date "from";
    - every figure is an as-of ledger or sub-ledger query by accounting date and owner book, with properties as a dimension.
- **The template:** `reporting.owner_statement` v1, in `packages/documents`, with times in the owner's zone.

### 4.4 Remittances

- **`remittance`** records the owner, amount, date, method, reference, proof, fees retained and who recorded it.
- **P16 posting:** Dr 1010 net, Dr 2300 fees retained, Cr 1070 total.
- **Fees taken alone** post Dr 2300, Cr 1070.
- **`owner_funding`** posts P30.

### 4.5 Dashboards and the rollup

- **`lease_snapshot`** (daily, per lease): paid-until, arrears by bucket, unearned rent, deposit held, rent roll and occupancy flags.
- **`metric_daily`** (per company and day): the Gate 1 figures and their inputs.
- **The dashboard** reads the snapshot, filtered by owner scope and by the DA-06 filters.
- **Exports:**
    - PDF through `DocumentsService`;
    - Excel through `write-excel-file`.

**Choices made where the plan was open.** Each is easy to reverse. The founder answered three of them; those answers follow the list.

*Fees*

1. **One-off charges carry no fee.** This includes termination charges (4200); OQ-31 names only rent and service charge.
2. **Surplus carries no fee,** even when P02 later draws it onto a month. *Reversed by the founder; see below.*
3. **The double-charge guard is optional, and the server should always pass it.** It stops two things being charged again:
    - withholding recorded after the cash paid the month (P19, M5 review 2);
    - credit a credit note released, when it is drawn onto a month again.

   Side effect: after a credit note releases rent already charged, the base charged stays above the rent settled by that amount until the credit pays a month again. If a new receipt comes first, its fee is lower by that amount.
4. **A refund counts released advance as refunded before surplus,** so the agent never keeps a fee on rent the owner gave back. Reversals round cumulatively half up, so the parts add up to the fee exactly.
5. **Fixed fees use the terms in force on the month's last day.**
    - Only lease days on or after those terms took effect count.
    - A switch from fixed to percentage during the month gives no fixed fee that month.
    - The lines carry property and unit, not a lease.
6. **A 0% rate is refused (use "none").** The ceiling is 10,000 bps.
7. **Reversal keys, proposed:** `P21:<payment>:fee` and `P14:<refund>:<payment>:refund`. A fee on a deposit applied, or on withholding recorded on its own, uses `P14:<record id>`.

*Remittances and the statement*

8. **Remittance lines sit at the owner's level,** with the property optional.
    - A remittance must pay the owner something; fees on their own use fees-taken lines (keyed P16).
    - Checks against the balances return problems, so the server decides whether to refuse or warn.
9. **Net remittable** is closing agent cash − reserve − fees due, never below zero. The reserve defaults to zero, and holding back rent tax payable was left open. *Answered by the founder; see below.*
10. **The agent section is on the summary page only:** settlements and remittances carry no property.
11. **Payment processing fees (5200; OQ-07) are added as an owner-level figure,** so gross collections reconcile to what was settled.
12. **Expenses by category are those recorded in the month, paid or not.** Those the agent paid also show as money out of 1070.
13. **Unearned service charge is shown beside unearned rent.**

*Dashboard*

14. **Lettable means every unit except those not lettable.** Reserved units and vacant units under repair count as vacant.
15. **Rent roll needs no division.** It is the monthly rent in force on the day (escalation steps applied) plus the monthly service charge, holding over included.
16. **Paid-until coverage takes months paid ahead as an input,** so `lease_snapshot` needs a months-paid-ahead column, which decision 11 does not list. A unit not paid ahead counts as zero, and the median is the lower middle.

*Gate 1*

17. **Collection rate:** a credit note reduces what is due only if dated by the month's end, and a write-off counts as not collected.
18. **Digital share:** the `test` method counts as digital, and payments are passed net of reversals.
19. **Auto-match:** a provider payment still unmatched counts against the rate.
20. **Time to receipt:** within one minute includes 60 s exactly. A receipt never sent counts as late but is left out of the median and p95. Percentiles are by nearest rank.
21. **Notice acknowledgement:**
    - 7 days are 168 hours from issue, inclusive;
    - a recipient still inside the window is pending, not missed;
    - a signature after the moment measured has not happened yet.
22. **Repair response:** what "attended" means was left open; M8 decision 4 says "respond" means assigned or attended. *Answered by the founder; see below.*

- **Period locks:** issuing locks the month; later entries take the open month's accounting date; a locked month's figures never change.
- **Gate 1 definitions** on fixed fixtures: the collection rate never exceeds 100%; and digital share, auto-match, time to receipt, notice acknowledgement and repair response.
- **Speed:** at 5,000 units, the dashboard answers within 1.5 s and a month's statement renders within 20 s. The rollup finishes within 10 minutes. All three run on the performance fixture, locally and in CI's integration job, with the speed check advisory in CI.
- **Exports:** the Excel and PDF exports match the screen's figures.
- **Cross-company isolation** for every new table.

## 7. New dependencies

None. Excel output uses `write-excel-file`, already in `packages/server`. PDFs use the existing renderer.

## 8. Risks

1. **Fee rules touch every receipt.** A mistake would misstate every owner's balance. The posting is property-tested, and the nightly checks gain "fees equal rate × base per receipt".
2. **Regeneration across locks:** any figure read by value date instead of accounting date would break it. Every statement query is written against accounting dates, and the regeneration test covers late corrections.
3. **Snapshot staleness:** the dashboard is up to a day old unless refreshed. The date and time it was computed are always shown.
4. **VAT on fees is unanswered (OQ-60).** Fees post gross. If VAT applies, a later rule row adds it without changing past fees.

### Backend

Each is easy to reverse unless it says otherwise.

*Fees and fee terms*

1. **Fee terms have their own table and approval.** Decision 1 said they would be a new version of the management agreement through `management_agreement.change`. They are `management_fee_terms` rows, changed through `management_fee_terms.change`:
    - subject: the owner;
    - payload: `{ownerId, ownerName, kind, rateBps, fixedMinor, graPaidBy, effectiveFrom, current}`.

   Why: the agreement records where rent settles, with M4b's 24-hour cooling-off, owner alert and provider subaccount. Fee terms have other approvers (the money approvers), no provider step, and must not disturb the settlement history.
    - One request at a time per owner.
    - `current` is read again at the decision, so a change in between cancels the request.
    - A start date that has passed by the time of approval becomes the day of approval, as M4b's agreements do. No backfill.
2. **Each fee is recorded in `management_fee`** (migrations 0050 and 0051; not in plan 4.6). Each row holds its base, rate, source (`payment`, `deposit`, `withholding`, `credit_draw`, `fixed`) and journal entry. A take-back is a negative row that references the original (`reversal`, `refund`, `unwind`). Two things read it:
    - the double-charge guard (domain choice 3);
    - the nightly check.
3. **Fee keys:**

   | Fee | Key |
   | --- | --- |
   | A receipt | `P14:<payment>` |
   | Tax withheld | `P14:<withholding>` |
   | Rent settled from a deposit | `P14:<move-out>` |
   | Credit drawn as a period starts | `P14:P02:<line>` |
   | Credit redrawn after a correction | `P14:P02:<line>:redraw:<n>` |
   | Credit applied at an early end or move-out | `P14:P13:<termination or move-out>:<key>` |
   | Fixed fee | `P14:<owner>:<unit>:<month>` |
   | Reversal mirror | `P21:<payment>:fee` |
   | Refund take-back | `P14:<termination or move-out>:<payment>:refund` |
   | Chargeback take-back of a deposit fee | `P14:<move-out>:<payment>:unwind` |

   A take-back row's own key is `<entry key>#<fee id>`.
4. **The guard covers the renewal chain,** not only the lease. Credit that a renewal carried over may already have been charged on the old lease, for example after a credit note released it. Integration tests cover single leases only; the chain path is untested.
5. **The fee hooks are registered when the fee service is constructed,** as rent tax's are. Both apps and the seed import `ReportingMoneyModule`. Test files charge fees only when they wire `reportingMoneyServices`, so every earlier suite runs as before.
6. **`reporting.fixed-fees` runs at 23:00 Accra from the 28th to the 1st.** The plan said "monthly, last day at 23:00". The company job posts on the company's own last day of a month, and on the 1st it posts last month again, which is a no-op by key. This covers a time zone ahead of Accra (a London owner in summer) and a failed run.

   A new job, `reporting.fee-check`, runs nightly at 03:15 (plan risk 1). It is read-only and raises the critical `reporting.fee_mismatch`. Both jobs are in the architecture job catalogue.

*Owner money*

7. **Remittances, fees taken and owner funding are refused, not warned about,** when `remittanceProblems` finds one: 409 `OVER_AGENT_CASH`, `FEES_OVER_DUE` or `INTO_RENT_TAX`.
    - A date in the future is 400 `DATE_IN_FUTURE`.
    - The checks run in the caller's scope before the proof is staged, and again in the write.
    - Writes for one owner take turns under an advisory lock, so two at once cannot together take more than the agent holds.
8. **Who may do what:** reads need `statements.read`, so owners and advisers read within their owner scope. Writes need `remittance.record`, and only an agency may record (409 `NOT_AN_AGENCY`). All of it is Growth only.

*Statements (the statements agent's choices)*

9. **Statement choices:**
    - **Template key:** `reporting.owner-statement`; the registry refuses underscores.
    - **The email** with the PDF is queued once the PDF is rendered, in the same transaction that records its hash. `delivered_at` is the moment it was queued.
    - **Who gets it:** the address on the owner's record. It goes with the `settlement_alert` purpose, because no `owner_statement` purpose exists yet; adding one needs a domain change and a migration.
    - **Scope:** a person limited to some properties sees an owner's statements only when every property the owner has held is in their scope. Drafting needs `statements.issue`.
    - **Arrears:** 1100 per invoice line past its due date at the month's end, so months of an annual invoice that have not started are not arrears. This differs from the dashboard's walk.
    - **Unearned rent:** the rest of 2010 after unearned service charge, credit not yet applied included.
    - **Collections:** each payment, match and reversal by its own accounting date.
    - **Occupancy:** from the units' status history as recorded by the month's end.
    - **Who pays GRA:** the fee terms in force on the month's last day; with none, the owner.
    - **Error codes:** 409 `STATEMENT_UNBALANCED`, with the checks that failed, and 409 `STATEMENT_NOT_ISSUED` on a draft's document route.

*Dashboards (the dashboard agent's choices)*

10. **Dashboard choices:**
    - **Gate 1 is company-wide,** so readers scoped to some owners or properties get none.
    - **Live figures:** occupancy and this month's collection rate are counted live, with filters and scope. Money figures, paid-until and expiries come from the snapshot.
    - **What the snapshot covers:** every lease holding a unit, plus every lease with money on it, the same set as the sub-ledger check.
    - **How Gate 1 picks its facts:** each metric's rules are in the dashboard agent's notes in `gate1.service.ts`. For example, a provider payment counts as automatic when its lease was known at receipt.
    - **Excel amounts** are written twice: as exact cedis text and as integer pesewas.
    - **Refresh** queues `reporting.metrics-refresh`, keyed per minute; it never runs inline.
