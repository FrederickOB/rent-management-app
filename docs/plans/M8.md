## 4. Design

### 4.1 Modules

- **`maintenance`** (layer 6): jobs and their state machine, artisans, job sheets, inspections and their templates, and expenses. It imports `tasks` for in-house assignment; the lint map adds that.
- **`moveout`** (layer 7, new): move-in reports and the move-out settlement. It imports `billing`, `approvals`, `esign`, `ledger` (through billing's services), `maintenance` and `documents`.
- **`tasks`** is widened to maintenance jobs: kinds `maintenance_attend` and `inspection_due`, and related type `maintenance_job`.
- **`messaging`** gains:
    - templates for the emergency alert, the job sheet link and the tenant's step updates;
    - staff WhatsApp consent;
    - the bot's "my repairs".

### 4.2 The job state machine

The machine follows architecture section 7, and its transitions live in `packages/domain/src/maintenance`:

- Reported → Triaged (priority and category set) → Assigned (staff or artisan) → In progress (before photo, or a reason) → Work done (after photo and completion note) → Closed (the tenant confirms, or no reply in 7 days).
- Work done → In progress (the tenant disputes); Assigned → Triaged (declined or unassigned); Reported or Triaged → Cancelled (duplicate or not needed, with a reason).
- Every step is a row in `maintenance_job_event` (append-only, with server time, actor and data). The job's status is derived and moved only by an audited trigger that checks the transition.

### 4.3 Repair requests

| Source | How it arrives | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tenant app | The repair screen: category, description, the "emergency" flag, and photos, video and voice notes. | Queued offline (decision 3); idempotent by a client id. |
| WhatsApp | M6's bot flow, now completed. | Media copied by M6's pipeline. |
| Staff and caretakers | The web board's "Log a repair", for any unit or common area. | The source records who logged it. |
| SMS and email | To the inbox; staff log it from the conversation. | Linked to the message. |

### 4.4 Alerts, SLAs and tenant updates

- **The emergency alert** (`maintenance.emergency-alert`, keyed by job id, 5 tries) goes on WhatsApp (template) and SMS to the property's managers and caretakers. Each recipient's delivery is recorded.
- **`maintenance.sla-check`** runs every 15 minutes. It warns once per job, target and stage (about to be missed, then missed).
- **Tenant updates** follow M6's channel order and consent: assigned, a visit planned, work done ("confirm or tell us what's wrong") and closed.

### 4.5 Artisans and job sheets

- **`artisan`:**
    - name, phone (E.164) and trades;
    - per-channel consent;
    - archived, never deleted.
- **The job sheet:**
    - a `secure_link` of purpose `job_sheet` (one artisan, one job), shown at `apps/links` `/j/[token]`;
    - it is server-rendered, under 1 MB and works on 3G;
    - **the artisan can:** accept or decline, record the start with a before photo, and record completion with after photos and a note;
    - a one-time code to the artisan's phone protects the completion (decision 6);
    - its uploads use the job-media purposes and limits.
- **What else changes:** the token redaction and the links proxy learn the `/j/` path.

### 4.6 Inspections

- **`inspection_template`** holds rooms and items per unit type, with company copies of the defaults. **`inspection`** records the unit, the lease, its kind, who did it, when it started and when it was submitted. **`inspection_item`** records the room, the item, its condition, a note and the matching move-in item.
- **`inspection_photo`** is append-only, with server time.
- **Once submitted,** an inspection is frozen (trigger). A correction is a new inspection that references the old one.

### 4.7 The move-in report (TL-08)

1. **The report:** the move-in inspection's submission freezes the template `inspection.move_in` v1 to a PDF. The renderer fetches photo thumbnails by document id.
2. **Signing:** the e-sign engine gains the subject `move_in_report`, completion `all_required`, signers are every tenant party plus the landlord's representative, and the consent is `move_in_report.v1`. Nothing else in the engine changes.
3. **Once everyone has signed:** the signed PDF joins the tenancy's documents, in the app and on request on WhatsApp.

### 4.8 The move-out settlement (TL-11, MI-11)

- **`move_out`**, one live per lease, records:
    - the planned date and its reason (end of term, tenant's notice, landlord's notice or early termination);
    - the inspection;
    - the settlement and its status: planned, inspected, settlement drafted, awaiting approval, approved, refund paid, closed.
- **`deposit_deduction`** is append-only. Each records:
    - its kind (unpaid rent or charges from the ledger, damage, cleaning, keys or missing items, other);
    - the amount;
    - the reason;
    - the inspection items and photos behind it.
- **The final statement** is a pure domain function with M5's figures plus the deposit. It shows:
    - the deposit held, the credit (prepayment) and the arrears;
    - the agreed charges (early), the break-clause credit (early, P27) and the deductions;
    - the deposit applied to arrears (P11), the deposit retained (P11, 4300) and the refund of credit (P13) and of the deposit (P12).

  It always sums to zero, which a property test checks.
- **Approval:** a new action type, `lease.move_out_settlement`. The approver sees the statement and every deduction's photos. Its `execute`, in one transaction:
    1. posts P01 and P27 (early);
    2. posts P13 (credit applied, then credit refund payable);
    3. posts P11 (deposit applied to arrears through allocations of source `deposit`, then damage to 4300);
    4. posts P12 (deposit refund payable, 2400);
    5. runs P17 on rent settled from the deposit.
- **The refund paid** (P12 and P13 paid) is recorded with its reference and proof.
- **The lease** becomes Ended, or Terminated for an early end. The unit falls vacant by PB-09's derivation.
- **The tenant** gets the final statement PDF on their channel.
- **M5's `early_termination` records** stay readable. New early ends go through `move_out`, and M5's screens become part of it.

### 4.9 Expenses (AC-03, decision 8)

- **`expense`** records:
    - the owner, the property and the unit (optional);
    - the category: repairs (5000); rates, insurance, utilities, cleaning, security, legal or other (5400);
    - the supplier name, the amount and the expense date;
    - the receipt document and the job (optional);
    - who paid: owner, agent or unpaid.
- **`expense_payment`** records a payable paid later.
- **Posting is P15,** with idempotency `P15:<expense>`. A correction is a reversal (P21a) and a new expense.
- **A job's cost** is the sum of its expenses.

### 4.10 Data model (migrations from 0040)

- `maintenance_job` gains:
    - priority, category, the tenant's emergency flag;
    - assignee (staff or artisan), target times;
    - before-photo reason, completion note;

- New tables:

    - `artisan`;
    - `inspection_template`, `inspection`, `inspection_item`, `inspection_photo`;
    - `move_out`, `deposit_deduction`;
    - `expense`, `expense_payment`;
    - staff consent rows (on the M6 consent tables).
- **Rules for every new table:**
    - RLS forced with property and owner scope, and composite company foreign keys;
    - append-only where the table holds evidence;
    - isolation tests.
- **The e-sign tables** gain the subject `move_in_report` and its foreign key.
- **`secure_link`** gains a target column for `job_sheet`.
- **Upload purposes:** `job_media` (photo, video, audio), `inspection_photo` and `expense_receipt`.

### 4.11 Screens

## 6. Tests

★ marks the playbook's and the PRD's.

    - a request from the app with photo, video and voice note creates one job with its files;
    - a retry, or an offline send, creates no second job;
    - on Starter the request is logged on the timeline and forwarded.
- **Media types:** files are checked by their first bytes (a renamed file is refused), with size and duration limits.
- **Job sheets:** the token serves one artisan and one job; completion needs the one-time code; the link expires when the job closes.
- **SLAs:** each warning goes once per target and stage.
- **Tenant confirmation and dispute;** auto-close after 7 days.
- **Inspections:**
    - a submitted inspection cannot change, and a correction references it;
    - the move-out inspection shows the move-in result beside each item.
- **The move-in report:** signed by every party; the PDF's hash verifies; it is listed in the tenant app.
- **P11 and P12:**
    - rent settled from the deposit is rent received (P17 follows);
    - damage goes to 4300;
    - deposits never touch income except as deductions retained.
- **Expenses (P15):** unpaid, paid by the agent and paid by the owner; a payable paid later; a reversal.
- **Cross-company isolation** for every new table.
- **Log redaction** for artisan phones and job-sheet tokens.
- **The job sheet page** stays under 1 MB.

Nothing else. File types are checked by their first bytes in our own code, and the web uses the browser's video and audio players.

## 8. Risks

1. **Phone storage and data cost.** Video on 3G is slow and costly. The limits (decision 2) and an upload progress bar help, and WhatsApp stays the light path.
2. **Merging M5's early termination into move-out** touches money code that has passed review. M5's tests stay, unchanged, and must keep passing.
3. **Photos in the move-in PDF** make it large. Thumbnails keep it under a few MB, and the full photos are linked by fingerprint.
4. **Deduction rules may be regulated.** No deposit law value is invented. New open question OQ-59 asks counsel whether Ghana law limits deposits, deductions or refund timing.
5. **Artisans' data:** names and phones are personal data (Act 843). Collect only those, record consent, and never log them.
