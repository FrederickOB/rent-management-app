## 2. The three rules the playbook asks me to state

**The recognition rule (from M4a, unchanged).**
- Rent and service charge are income in the month they cover.
- On the first day of each monthly charge period, that month's line becomes income. It is paid first from money allocated to that line in advance, then from the lease's credit (oldest payment first), and the rest is owed.
- An advance is therefore recognised one month at a time. What has not been recognised is unearned rent, a liability (2010).

**The paid-until rule for partial months (from M4a, unchanged).**
- Paid-until is the last day of the last month whose rent and service-charge lines are fully covered, in an unbroken run from the billing start. A line counts as covered by allocations if it has started, or by advance allocations plus the credit applied forward in accrual order if it has not.
- A month that is only partly covered does not move paid-until. It shows as "plus GH₵X towards the month starting …".
- Rent periods are anchored on the lease start, so only a short final period is ever prorated.

**The rent tax basis (decision D9, unverified).**
- **When:** rent tax is due on rent **received**, advances included, per receipt.
- **The base:** the receipt's rent share, from the same forward walk, plus its service-charge share while `rent_tax.includes_service_charge` is on.
- **Never taxed:** deposits, VAT, and credit beyond the lease end.
- **Rate:** the rate for the lease's use: 8% residential, 15% commercial.
- **Rounding:** half up, once per receipt.
- **Due:** 30 days after the value date.
- **Status:** every one of these values is in the rules table, marked `from_prd` or `unverified`, and the tax adviser confirms them (OQ-17 to OQ-19).

## 5. Design

### 5.1 Rent tax (P17, TX-01, TX-02)

- **The tax item.** When a payment posts (P03, P06 or P28), the tax module computes the receipt's taxable base from the forward walk (section 2), applies the rate as of the value date, and rounds half up once per receipt. It writes:
    - one `rent_tax_item` for the receipt, with its base, rate, amount and due date;
    - the rule versions it used;
    - its lines, split across the invoice lines by largest remainder, so they add up exactly.

    In the same transaction it posts P17: Dr 5300, Cr 2200.
- **The cap.** A line's tax across all its receipts never exceeds the rate times the line's amount.
- **Credit applied later.** Credit beyond the lease end is not taxed at receipt. If it is later carried to a renewal (P29) and applied to rent, it is taxed then.
- **A payment reversal** (P21) mirrors the payment's P17 entries, and its tax items show as reversed.
- **Status of an item:** open, part paid, paid to GRA, withheld by the tenant, or reversed.
- **`tax.rent-tax-alerts`** (daily 08:00) raises the alerts at 21 and 28 days for open items (decision 5).

### 5.2 Withholding (P19, P20, TX-04)

- **Recording.** Withholding is recorded with a payment (the tenant paid net) or later (the tenant advised it afterwards). Each needs the amount and the lines it was withheld on. P19 posts Dr 1150, Cr 1100 on those lines, with the allocation source "withholding".
- **The tax on those lines becomes the amount withheld.** P17 accrues the withheld amount, rent tax already accrued on their cash is reversed, and the tax item is marked "withheld by tenant", so TX-02 raises no alerts.
- **The certificate** is uploaded by a manager and confirmed by a landlord user (decision 3). P20 then posts:
    - for an owner outside the letting business: Dr 2200, Cr 1150 (the withholding settles the rent tax);
    - for a letting-business owner: Dr 1160, Cr 1150 (a tax credit).
- **No certificate:** one that never comes is written off with two-person approval (P24: Dr 5900, Cr 1150).
- **Rates** come from `withholding_tax.rate.*` (unverified).

### 5.3 GRA payments and the rent tax report (P18, TX-03)

- **A GRA payment:** date, amount, GRA receipt number, the receipt file, and the tax items it settles (oldest first by default, adjustable). It posts P18: Dr 2200, Cr 1010 or 1070.
- **The rent tax report** for an owner and a period selects P17 entries, net of reversals, by accounting date (invariant 6). For each receipt it shows the base, rate, tax, due date and status, then totals, GRA payments and the balance outstanding.
- **Regeneration:** posted history never changes and locked months take no new entries, so a report for a past month regenerates identically.
- **Formats:** on screen, as a PDF through the documents service, and as CSV.

### 5.4 Early termination (AR-04, P13)

When a move-out ends a lease before its end date (M3's minimal move-out, or M8's full one):
1. **Charges and credit:**
    - agreed termination charges are raised as one-off lines (P01);
    - where the break clause allows (`creditUnusedDays` in the M3 terms), the unused days of the current month are credited (P27). Otherwise the month is not prorated (prd-issues 36).
2. **Future lines:** M4a has already cancelled lines for periods after the end, so their money is back in credit.
3. **Open lines:** the prepayment is applied to open lines, oldest first (P13 apply).
4. **The refund:** a positive remainder, less deductions approved on the request, is raised as `lease.refund` with two-person approval. On approval P13 posts Dr 2010, Cr 2400. Paying it, outside the platform with a reference and proof, posts Dr 2400, Cr 1010 or 1070.
5. **Lines still open** stay as arrears: settle them from the deposit (P11, M8), collect them, or write them off (P24).

### 5.5 Advance cap and paid-until prompt (AR-05, AR-06)

- **Months paid ahead.** Each payment records how many months beyond the current period it pays, from the walk at receipt.
- **Under `warn` or `block`,** a payment beyond `advance_rent.max_months` for the lease's term:
    - flags the lease;
    - appears in the compliance report (lease, months paid ahead, the cap, the rule version and its status).

    The product never calls an advance compliant.
- **Under `block`:** decision 4.
- **`billing.paid-until-prompt`** (daily 08:00) finds leases whose paid-until is 90 days away and prompts as decision 5, once per lease and paid-until date.

### 5.6 VAT returns (P33)

- **The report:** per owner and month, the VAT, NHIL and GETFund charged at their tax points (M4a's P32), by invoice.
- **A VAT payment to GRA:** recorded with the return period and the GRA receipt (P33: Dr 2210, Cr 1010 or 1070).
- **Not built:** filing itself, and E-VAT invoicing with GRA (OQ-20).

### 5.7 Tax rules (TX-08)

- **The rules read:** every rate, due-day count, alert day, base option and cap is read from the rules table as of the business date. The rule version is recorded on each tax item, VAT line and AR-05 flag.
- **A future-dated rate** is a new row; it changes nothing before its effective date (★ test).
- **The warning:** unverified rows show staff the warning on every screen and report that uses them.

### 5.8 Screens

- **Rent tax:**
    - by owner and period: the receipts with their tax, due dates and status;
    - the due list with the 21- and 28-day alerts;
    - "Record a GRA payment";
    - downloads: PDF and CSV.
- **Withholding:** on the payment and the lease, record withholding, upload a certificate, and confirm it as a landlord user.
- **Early termination:** on the lease, after a move-out: the calculation shown step by step (charges, credit, prepayment applied, refund and deductions), then "Request the refund".
- **Compliance:** the advance-cap report under `warn` or `block`.
- **VAT:** the report per owner and month, and "Record a VAT payment".
- **Lease page:** rent tax on each payment, withholding status, the AR-05 flag and the AR-06 prompt.

## 5.11 Progress

**2026-10-30, groundwork (committed on `m5-tax` while M4a was being built).**
- **Domain:** `packages/domain/src/tax` (rent tax per receipt, due and alert dates, withholding checks, the certificate's account and confirmer), `billing/termination.ts` (unused-day credits, credit applied to open lines, the refund), `billing/advance-cap.ts` (months paid ahead, the most a payment may be under `block`), and `ledger/tax-posting.ts` (P13 apply, approve and pay; P17; P18; P19; P20; P24 on withholding; P33).
- **The receipt's base, made precise** (it refines 5.1's "cap" and "credit applied later" without changing the basis): a receipt is taxed on the rent it newly covers, which is the forward walk's covered rent and service charge after the receipt less the base already taxed on the lease. That base is pinned to the periods it newly covers.
    - **Why:** taxing each receipt on its own share of the walk, with a per-line cap, under-taxes when a later payment pays invoices that an earlier payment's credit was expected to cover. The earlier credit moves to later months, and those months were never taxed. Measuring the increase in what the lease's money covers taxes the later payment on the months the credit moved to.
    - **Proved** by property tests over M4a's random leases: without corrections, the base taxed on a lease equals the rent its money covers after every receipt. With reversals and credit notes, no receipt is taxed beyond its own money and no lease beyond what it received.
    - **Corrections:** a reversal mirrors its payment's tax (P21) and a credit note leaves tax in place (OQ-19). Either can leave the taxed base above or below what is covered; the next receipts catch up, within their own money.
    - Money beyond the term is not rent, so it is untaxed until something applies it to rent (a renewal, P29). That application is taxed then.
